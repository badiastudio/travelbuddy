Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 17, 2026

Introduction

travelBuddy ("we", "our", "the app") is a travel planning app that helps you organize trips, track expenses, and collaborate with fellow travelers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address, display name, and (optionally) profile photo when you register.

Trip Data: Trip details, stops, itinerary, expenses, packing lists, photos, documents, and messages you create in the app.

Location Data: GPS coordinates only when you tag a stop with a location. We do not track your location in the background.

Usage Data: Standard app diagnostic data (crashes, performance) collected by Apple TestFlight and the App Store.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core features (trip planning, sharing, syncing)
  • To authenticate you securely
  • To allow you to share trips with people you invite
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely with Supabase (our backend provider) using industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Photos and documents are stored in encrypted cloud storage. Row-level security policies ensure that only you and members of trips you're invited to can access your trip data.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage
  • Google Maps — location search and map display
  • Apple / Google — push notifications, app distribution

Your Rights

  • Access: View all your data in the app at any time
  • Deletion: Delete your account by contacting us at cshunnarah@gmail.com — all your data will be permanently removed within 30 days
  • Export: Use the "Export PDF" feature to download your trip data
  • Correction: Edit any information directly in the app

Children's Privacy

travelBuddy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the app or email.
