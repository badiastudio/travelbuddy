## Full Description

**travelBuddy** is your all-in-one travel planning companion. Whether you're planning a weekend getaway or a month-long adventure across continents, travelBuddy helps you organize every detail and share the experience with fellow travelers.

**Plan Every Detail**
📍 Build day-by-day itineraries with categorized stops
🗺️ See all your stops on an interactive map
📅 Timeline view of your entire trip
🧳 Smart packing list suggestions based on destination and duration
✅ Pre-trip checklist so nothing gets forgotten

**Travel Together**
👥 Invite friends and family to your trips
💬 Group chat for each trip
👍 Vote on stops as a group
🧾 Split expenses fairly with automatic balance tracking
📸 Share photos and documents in one place

**Track Every Dollar**
💳 Log expenses in any currency
📊 Budget tracking with spending alerts
🧮 Automatic bill splitting
💰 See who owes whom at a glance

**Discover More**
🏛️ Find nearby attractions, restaurants, and hotels
🔗 Save booking links for each stop
📄 Export your trip as a PDF

**Free & Private**
🔒 Your data is encrypted and secure
✨ No ads, ever
🌍 Works on iPhone and Android

Perfect for family vacations, friend group trips, honeymoons, business travel, and solo adventures.
